How It Works

Read. Decide. Justify. Get diagnosed.

  • Face a real workplace scenario

    Not a textbook question. A real situation — you've been asked to use AI on confidential client work, deadline in 2 hours. What do you do?

    Anyone can pick the right answer. We capture WHY you picked it. Your reasoning reveals whether you truly understand the risk.
