About ThinkPrint

Judgment Is the New Job Skill

Anyone can list skills on a CV. ThinkPrint verifies how people actually think under realistic workplace pressure.

Why We Built This

Every week another company rolls out AI tools to their workforce. Every week the same thing happens — someone makes a bad call. Sends client data to ChatGPT. Publishes AI-generated content without checking it. Bypasses a policy because a deadline is tight.

The problem is not that people don't know the rules. They've read the policy. They've done the training. The problem is that when a real situation happens — fast, pressured, ambiguous — knowledge doesn't automatically become judgment.

ThinkPrint was built to close that gap. We test judgment — not knowledge. We verify how people actually reason when it matters. And we give them a professional diagnosis and a credential that proves it.
