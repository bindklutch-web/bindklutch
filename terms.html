Your assessment responses are used to generate your AI-powered report. Your email is used for account communication. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

Your assessment responses are processed by the Claude AI API (Anthropic) to generate your report. Anthropic's privacy policy governs this processing. We send only your anonymised responses — not your name or email — to the AI system.
